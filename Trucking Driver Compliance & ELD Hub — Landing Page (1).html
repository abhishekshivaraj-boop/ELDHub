Stop chasing records across spreadsheets

Driver credentials, renewal dates, HOS notes and inspection outcomes often live in separate files. That makes expiring licenses easy to miss and past inspections hard to find. The hub puts them side by side and shows what needs attention first.

Everything you need to track driver compliance

Six focused modules that cover the first release of the product.

Compliance dashboard

See total drivers, expired licenses, licenses due soon and records waiting for review at a glance.

Driver management

Create, edit and list driver profiles with validated required fields.

License renewal tracking

Store each CDL number and expiry date. Status is calculated from the date, with a 30-day alert window you can change.

HOS audit logs

Record event type, timestamps, notes and review status for each hours-of-service entry.

DOT inspection records

Log date, driver, vehicle, result, notes and a document reference for every inspection.

Search and filters

Find records by driver name, license number, status or expiry range.

Common questions

 

Does this connect to ELD devices?

Not in the first release. The hub stores and organizes records. It does not read data from certified ELD hardware or telematics providers.

Does it guarantee DOT or FMCSA compliance?

No. It helps your team see and manage records. Legal compliance remains the responsibility of your organization.

How does license status work?

Each driver has an expiry date. The system derives Expired, Due soon or Valid from that date, so status is never entered by hand.

Which database does it use?

To be confirmed. The data model is designed to work with MongoDB, PostgreSQL or MySQL.
